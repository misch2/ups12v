## Charging authorization truth table

Actual topology: R9 10k pulls CE_N toward CE_BIAS; D3/D4 diode-OR REGN/MAIN_3V3 into that bias. Q3 PMV16UN sinks CE_N when its gate is sourced by Q5. R10 10k discharges Q3 gate. Q5 PNP emitter is MAIN_3V3; R44 100k restores its base to emitter. R53 10k limits Q5 base current through series Q6/Q7. Q6 base is driven by MAIN through R50 100k. Q7 base is GPIO0 through R46 4k7 with R47 100k to PGND. The old J4 CHG_REQ is not an authorization path: R11 is DNP and its second pad is no-connect.

| State | Q7 / Q6 / Q5 / Q3 | CE / result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No power | Off / off / off / off | CE has no guaranteed high voltage without bias; charger is unpowered, no charging. |
| Normal power, MCU not booted, GPIO high impedance | Off by R47 / main-biased / off by R44 / off by R10 | R9 holds CE high once charger-owned REGN bias is valid; disabled. Bias-before-charge timing remains a prototype check. |
| MCU reset or GPIO high impedance | Off / main-biased / off / off | Disabled after transistor/gate discharge; not a claimed zero-delay reset interlock. Firmware must leave GPIO0 low/input until authorization checks pass. |
| USB only, no SYS/MAIN, even if GPIO requests charge | May turn on / off / no emitter supply / off | No main-domain sink path or Q3 gate drive; charger unpowered; USB does not authorize charging. |
| Normal domain established, firmware explicitly high | On / on / on / on | CE low, charging allowed subject to charger settings, NTC, pack and source limits. |
| MAIN supply lost while GPIO stays high via USB | On / loses bias / loses emitter supply / gate discharges | CE released, pulled high if REGN still exists; no charging after MAIN collapses. During decay, disable voltage/timing is analog and unqualified. |

Loss of adapter alone while a battery still maintains SYS/MAIN is not loss of the normal domain. It removes input energy, but does not by itself force this gate off; firmware must handle source validity. The design intentionally has no stored authorization, precision MAIN-valid threshold, latch, timeout or supervisor. In particular, Q6/Q8 labels saying MAIN_VALID do **not** establish 3.0 V qualification. The TPS2116 PR1 threshold controls source selection only. A requirement that CE release at a precise MAIN voltage would be a changed requirement; no such threshold is invented here.

At established MAIN, nominal Q6 base bias is around 25 uA and Q5 base path is around 0.2 mA; Q5 drives approximately 0.34 mA into R10 plus Q3 gate charge. This is a plausibility calculation, not transistor minimum-gain/temperature or dynamic proof. Keep exact BC847/BC857 suffix sourcing and reset/power-down CE waveform qualification open. BQ25792 can revert to 2 A on charger-only reset/watchdog expiry while MCU still holds CE low; firmware's 0.5 A target is not a hardware ceiling.

## Supplies, mux and recovery

Manufacturer basis, re-opened on 2026-10-10: [TI TPS62933 SLUSEA4D Rev. D, Aug 2022](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62933.pdf), §§7, 8.3/8.5, 9.3.5–9.3.7, 10.2; [TPS2116 SLVSFG1A Rev. A, May 2021](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf), §§5–7.6; [AP7361C DS37274 Rev. 5-2, Oct 2020](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/AP7361C.pdf), pin/electrical/thermal tables and application section. Detailed thermal assumptions remain in [LDO selection](phase3-4-usb-ldo-selection.md). Calculated values are in the verification JSON; none are hardware measurements.

- **U3:** SYS input 6–18 V lies within the IC's 3.8–30 V range. R32/R33 = 32.2k/10k, 0.1%, gives **3.376 V nominal**, not exactly 3.300 V. Full-temperature VFB, resistor and ±0.15 uA leakage screen gives **3.299–3.454 V**. Conservatively allowing 74 mOhm mux loss at 1 A gives **3.225 V minimum before PCB/ripple**, above 3.135 V steady specification. At the high corner, only about **11 mV** remains to 3.465 V (+5%); ripple/regulation/trace budget needs confirming before supply freeze. This is a real narrow margin, not an absolute-maximum violation (module limit 3.6 V).
- **U3 startup/current:** R36/R37 100k/38.3k gives about 4.30 V rising / 4.01 V falling using typical EN currents; even 1.28 V rising threshold gives about 4.55 V before resistor/current uncertainty, below supported SYS 6 V. EN is about 5.04 V at SYS 18 V using typical enabled pull-up current, below 5.5 V. The IC's 30 V input rating does not authorize 30 V on this assembled EN divider. RT floats for 500 kHz; C39 33n gives approximately 4.8 ms soft-start; C37 100n is BST-to-SW. L2 4.7uH gives about 1.17 A p-p at 18 V / nominal frequency, peak about 1.58 A at a 1 A load. Recorded XAL5030-472MEC current class has margin; exact saturation/loss/Ceff/PCB thermals remain qualification. C32/C43 input and C33/C45 output return to PGND. Their final voltage/DC-bias grades are not yet encoded by exact MPNs; require >=25 V-class normal input capacitors with adequate effective capacitance under the approved SYS envelope, and adequate output Ceff per reference design. No measured 1 A delivery is claimed.
- **U4:** correct E package pin map and SOT-223 ground tab. C27 4.7u + C28 100n input, C35 22u + C40 100n output exceed nominal datasheet minima 1u/2.2u; verify effective output capacitance when choosing parts. USB-only startup depends on no charger/battery or firmware handshake. 300 mA dropout maximum 140 mV leaves useful cable headroom at the stated 4.35 V input screening assumption. At 5.25 V input and 50 C ambient/minimum-land 110 C/W, calculated junction is **116.6 C at 300 mA**, **160.9 C at 500 mA continuous**. Therefore 500 mA hot continuous service is not qualified, nor is 1 A operation. ROM load, USB allowance, inrush and real thermal copper must be measured; disabling application Wi-Fi does not prove the ROM current budget.
- **U5:** VIN1/MODE = MAIN, VIN2 = USB3V3, VOUT = MCU3V3. R23/R24 8.2k/4.7k give PR1 = 1.202 V at MAIN 3.3 V, nominal MAIN selection threshold **2.745 V**, and approximately **2.493–3.003 V** corner range using reference tolerance, 1% resistors and ±0.1uA PR1 leakage. At regulated MAIN, PR1 is independent of VOUT and comfortably above the largest boundary, so there is no DC source-selection feedback loop. There is no established guaranteed PR1 hysteresis/no-oscillation margin under a current-limited or marginal main source; qualify source ramps/steps. Break-before-make and fast-collapse behavior may reset MCU, as accepted. No hold-up/supervisor is added.
- **Recovery:** U1 EN uses R16 10k/C29 1u and SW2 to ground; GPIO9 BOOT uses R15/SW1, GPIO2/8 use R17/R18 straps. Cold restart has an RC and fixed boot straps rather than a latch that requires a prior firmware edge. Native USB GPIO18/19 remain available. Supported recovery is structurally present, but reliable cold-ROM flash and complete-interruption restart remain **unmeasured**, using the already archived Espressif module v2.2 guidance as source. Never describe a netlist as a physical recovery test.

## USB, signal isolation and back-power

J7 A6/B6 -> USB_DP -> R48 -> U1 GPIO19; A7/B7 -> USB_DN -> R49 -> U1 GPIO18. U2 TPD2EUSB30A shunts DP/DN to PGND without a rail-clamp path into unpowered MAIN. Both CC pins have independent 5k1 1% Rd resistors, SBU pins no-connect, VBUS feeds only C27/C28/U4. BOOT held low across RESET selects ROM recovery. UART_TX/RX currently terminate only as labels on module pins: physical access is still missing. The optional 22R substitution for the fitted 0R USB series parts is an SI qualification option, not an automatic change.

[TI TCA9517 SCPS242D Rev. D, July 2017](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9517.pdf), §§9.3 and 11, supports powered-off high-impedance I2C and either supply sequence. U6 A supply/pull-ups = MAIN and B = MCU; retain **original non-A** TCA9517DGKR despite the inherited symbol library name TCA9517ADGK. Q8 gate sees MAIN; with MAIN absent Q8 is off, R51 drives Q9 on and Q9 holds EN low. With MAIN present Q8 pulls NEN down and Q9 releases EN for MCU-side R52/internal pull-up. Thus EN's internal MCU-domain pull-up does not conduct into MAIN. EN transitions should occur with idle buses; firmware must recover I2C after supply changes. Existing 4k7 pull-ups and A/B low-level margins are retained; start at 100 kHz and qualify faster timing.

Q4 isolates main INT into an MCU-side pulled-up collector; firmware must account for inversion. Charge request drives only Q7 base, not a main-rail pull-up. D3/D4 prevent a forward DC path from CE_BIAS into the other supply. TPS2116 reverse blocking prevents a deliberate conducting USB->MAIN or MAIN->USB power path. There is no intentional USB VBUS path to SYS, BATTERY, VIN, REGN or PMID. J4 belongs to the normal domain and is not a USB-powered UART header; any external harness must respect those domains.

This is a **static topology pass**, not a proof of zero current. TPS2116 reverse blocking has finite thresholds/leakage; AP7361C has no established reverse-current protection. R35 10k bleeds USB3V3 (0.33 mA at 3.3 V), but does not guarantee safe output-to-input current on USB removal. Capacitor discharge, powered-off I2C/INT leakage, CE transitions and both mux directions remain matching-prototype measurements. Keep the existing 10 uA target without inventing additional ICs because an aggregate limit is not yet proven.
