# UPS4 physical charge-enable investigation — 2026-11-10

Read-only investigation and implementation proposal for owner review. No CAD or firmware was changed, compiled or flashed; no MQTT connection, command or charge-enable action was performed. New files beside this report are analysis exports/check results only. Existing local edits were preserved. The supplied TP14 measurement and earlier MQTT captures are historical observations, not measurements repeated during this investigation.

**Finding:** the saved circuit requires **U1 module pad 12 / ESP32-C3 GPIO0 (`MCU_CHG_ARM`) HIGH** to pull **IC1 pad 13 / `CE_N` LOW**. Current ups4 source never configures or drives that request. TP14=4.5 V is consistent with the intended default-off circuit. It proves a physical charging inhibit, but does not prove all driver components work or exclude another inhibit after CE is lowered.

## 1. Actual circuit and polarity

Connectivity below comes from the fresh exported netlist, corroborated against saved PCB pads. Voltages are **calculated/expected steady-state values**, relative to PGND, not new measurements. Approximate transistor junction voltages are diagnostic guides rather than guaranteed limits.

| Node / parts | Exact connection and function | Request OFF | Request ON, MAIN_3V3 present |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MCU_CHG_ARM` | U1.12 GPIO0 → R46.1; MCU3V3 domain, normally about 3.3 V | GPIO LOW or high impedance | GPIO about MCU3V3 |
| `MCU_CHG_BASE` | R46=4.7k → Q7.1 base; R47=100k to PGND; Q7.2 emitter=PGND | About 0 V through R47 | About 0.6–0.8 V; Q7 ON |
| `CHG_AND` | Q7.3 collector → Q6.2 emitter | Floating/history-dependent; do not assign a required LOW | Near ground, typically roughly 0.05–0.2 V |
| Q6 main bias | MAIN_3V3 → R50=100k → Q6.1 base | With Q7 off, emitter/base can float up; no sustained sink chain | Base about CHG_AND + one VBE, roughly 0.7–1.0 V |
| `/CHG_BASE` | Q6.3 collector → R53.1 | Near MAIN_3V3 through the Q5 base network, subject to leakage | Low, above Q7 collector by Q6 saturation voltage |
| `/Q5_BASE_LIMITED` | R53=10k → Q5.1 base; R44=100k from MAIN_3V3 to Q5 base; Q5.2 emitter=MAIN_3V3 | Base near emitter, Q5 OFF | About MAIN_3V3 − 0.6–0.8 V; Q5 PNP ON |
| `/CHG_GATE` | Q5.3 collector → Q3.1 gate; R10=10k to PGND | About 0 V; Q3 OFF | Near MAIN_3V3, approximately 3.1–3.3 V; Q3 ON |
| `CE_BIAS` | D3 anode=REGN; D4 anode=MAIN_3V3; both cathodes join CE_BIAS; D3/D4 BAS116H | Diode OR of available charger/main rails | Same bias source; diode drop changes with current |
| `CE_N`, TP14 | CE_BIAS → R9=10k → IC1.13 CE; Q3.3 drain at CE_N; Q3.2 source=PGND | Approximately higher available bias rail minus diode drop; 4.5 V plausible with REGN active | Near PGND, must measure ≤0.4 V |

The complete enable path is therefore:

`GPIO0 HIGH → Q7 NPN ON → Q6 NPN sinks through R53 → Q5 PNP sources MAIN_3V3 → Q3 NMOS ON → CE_N LOW`.

Q6 is biased by the **raw MAIN_3V3 rail**, not an adapter-valid comparator or supervisor. Battery operation can also power MAIN_3V3, so this hardware condition does not identify external input power. Q7 isolates the MCU request from charger/main supply nodes; Q3 isolates its 3.3 V gate from the higher CE bias. No normal DC connection carries CE_N's 4.5 V into GPIO0. Leakage, shorts and switching transients remain bench questions.

**`CHG_REQ` is a different net:** J4.5 → R11.1; **R11.2 is explicitly unconnected**. It has no connection to GPIO0 or the CE driver. Driving J4.5 cannot enable charging. Do not use it as an enable input or short TP14 to ground.

At a nominal GPIO=3.3 V, Q7 base drive is approximately `(3.3−0.7)/4.7k − 0.7/100k = 0.55 mA`. Q6 base drive is approximately `(3.3−0.8)/100k = 25 µA`; its collector load through R53 is roughly 0.2–0.25 mA. Q5 drives roughly 0.3 mA through R10 after charging Q3's gate. Q3 sinks roughly 0.45 mA with CE_BIAS=4.5 V. These estimates support the intended logic; they are not worst-case qualification over temperature and rail ramps.

Exact netlist locations: [Q3](ce-path-review-20261110.net:7475), [Q5/Q6/Q7](ce-path-review-20261110.net:7649), [driver collector/gate nets](ce-path-review-20261110.net:15870), [Q5 base](ce-path-review-20261110.net:16183), [CE bias](ce-path-review-20261110.net:16371), [CE_N](ce-path-review-20261110.net:16393), [CHG_AND](ce-path-review-20261110.net:16421), [service CHG_REQ](ce-path-review-20261110.net:16438), [GPIO0 request/base](ce-path-review-20261110.net:16906), [Q6 base](ce-path-review-20261110.net:17280). ## 2. Reset, boot, operation and power loss

| Condition | Expected circuit behavior |
|---|---|
| Hardware reset / ROM boot, main or REGN bias present | GPIO0 not actively HIGH; R47 holds Q7 off, R44 holds Q5 off, R10 discharges Q3 gate; CE_N HIGH after bias settles. GPIO0 is not an ESP32-C3 boot-strapping pin. Scope actual reset and boot transitions. |
| Present source, normal current firmware | No GPIO0 output initialization or drive exists. Default-off is expected; measured TP14 HIGH agrees. EN_CHG can nevertheless be ON. |
| Proposed permitted charging | GPIO0 HIGH and main power available → CE_N LOW; charging still depends on EN_CHG and charger qualifications. |
| Proposed ordinary inhibit / error | Drive GPIO0 LOW first → CE_N HIGH while bias is available, then attempt EN_CHG=0 over I2C. |
| MCU loses power, main/REGN remain | GPIO drive disappears; R47/R44/R10 restore default-off. Check discharge timing and leakage physically. Removing service USB alone does not depower an MCU still supplied from MAIN through U5. |
| MAIN disappears but USB keeps MCU alive | Q5 loses its source and R50 loses bias; R10 turns Q3 off. CE HIGH if REGN/CE bias remains. A retained GPIO HIGH can re-enable immediately when MAIN returns; no fresh-edge latch exists. |
| All main/REGN bias absent, USB only | CE_BIAS is not supplied by USB3V3. CE_N may be near 0 V; the unpowered charger's CE level is not evidence of permitted charging. |
| MCU stall while GPIO remains HIGH | Hardware permission may remain asserted indefinitely. This circuit is default-off on loss of drive/power, not an independent firmware-liveness interlock. |

Do not extend the hardware-reset expectation to every CPU-only reset, GPIO hold state, brownout ramp or panic interval without measurement. No guaranteed startup/off delay follows from the static netlist.

## 3. Discrepancies and unverified assembly

1. **Missing implementation:** schematic U1 metadata describes firmware leaving CHG_ARM low until configuration, but the source has no CE pin field or drive. [Config::Pins](../../../software/ups/include/config.h:10) contains only I2C and LED fields; [ups4](../../../software/ups/include/boards/v4/ups4_esp76/board_config.h:10) declares only those. [setup](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:1045) initializes LED/I2C/network before charger service.
2. **Current differs from hardware plan:** ups4 explicitly requests **1000 mA**, not the architectural nominal 500 mA. The [saved input assessment](mqtt-ups4-pd12v-20261110-161616/ASSESSMENT.md:35) corroborates 1000 mA register readback. No permission to expose this setting to a battery follows from EN_CHG already being ON.
3. **Approved Q3 substitution:** CAD/export says PMV16UN; [assembly row 81](../sourcing/FINAL_ASSEMBLY_BOM.csv:81) specifies **AO3400A**, same SOT-23 G1/S2/D3 arrangement. This is a documented substitution, not evidence of incorrect routing. Verify the installed marking/orientation. Q5 is BC857B,215 PNP; Q6/Q7 are BC847B,215 NPN ([rows 83–85](../sourcing/FINAL_ASSEMBLY_BOM.csv:83)); their pins are B1/E2/C3. All driver resistors and D3/D4 are specified fitted. The U1 plan uses the approved H4X module despite older N4X naming in CAD.
4. **R11 documentation conflict:** older BOM review called it DNP; the authoritative population lists and final assembly BOM say fitted. Its open second pad makes either population electrically unable to enable charging. Verify that no assembly bridge or external modification connects that pad elsewhere.
5. **Temperature:** fitted owner-reported MF52-103 B3950 differs from the source's default beta 3435 ([config](../../../software/ups/include/config.h:41)); near room temperature the earlier assessment calculated only about 0.1°C impact. This does not resolve absolute temperature or qualify the cell charging window. Preserve R6=5.6k/R7=33k and autonomous TS protection; verify the actual divider, thermistor and attachment.
6. No CE-path schematic/PCB connectivity discrepancy was found. Approved population records do not prove actual ups4 soldered population. R60/R61's known assembly-versus-CAD values are outside the CE path and remain a separate recorded issue.

## 4. Existing charging control and gaps

- **Historical running capture / initial source: Legacy.** Saved MQTT reports `charge_profile=Legacy` and VREG=16800 mV. [checkChargerStatus](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:326), called every 30 s, uses PG, not-backup and pack-average voltage. The initially inspected ups4 source's two 9999 mV thresholds effectively requested charging whenever its input condition was true. This is not individual-cell protection; the legacy condition also lacks an explicit OTG exclusion and does not clear EN_CHG when input disappears.
- **Final current source: APPROX_80.** The concurrent [board edit](../../../software/ups/include/boards/v4/ups4_esp76/board_config.h:25) selects 3920 mV/cell and removes those threshold overrides. With CELL=4S, the managed target is **15680 mV**; recharge offset remains inherited/default zero (preserve the chip setting). This selects the managed path below and bypasses the legacy 30-second enable policy. It still supplies no physical CE control or temperature qualification. The label does not demonstrate 80% SOC for the actual cells, and no running-device readback was taken after this edit.
- **Managed FULL/APPROX_80:** [applyChargeProfile](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:358) and [ChargeControl::apply](../../../software/ups/include/charge_profile.h:52) validate identity, planned voltage/recharge and readback, then may set EN_CHG when PG is good and status is not OTG/backup. They neither drive CE nor qualify NTC temperature/current limits. Passing `forwardPower=false` prevents a new enable but does not clear an already-set EN_CHG. Failure's EN_CHG=0 write is best effort only.
- `batteryCellCount()` derives series count from the chip ([UPS.ino:193](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:193)); feeding that same value back into the profile is not an independent check that the physical pack is 4S. An explicit configured expected count is needed for ups4.
- [Initialization](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:379) can skip current/input setup when VAC2_ADC_DIS is set. It can reset the charger before disabling EN_CHG, and disables the charger watchdog. Managed readback verifies voltage/CELL/recharge, not the complete current/temperature/input safety configuration. A future CE enable must not trust the marker.
- [Error handling](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:183) clears the library error and marks the charger unavailable; [setChargerReady](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:475) does not revoke physical permission or reliably turn charging off. [serviceCharger](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:494) polls each main loop and retries discovery after 5 s. A partial failed register read leaves a mixed/stale cache; it must not authorize charging.
- Library setters update their cache before I2C completion: [BQ25798Core.h:286](../../../software/ups/.pio/libdeps/ups4/BQ25798/lib/BQ25798Core/BQ25798Core.h:286). Immediate getters are not independent readback. Bulk reads and sticky errors are at lines 264 and 551. Re-read hardware and require successful completion before trusting a configuration.
- [sendCalculatedValues](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:291) calculates NTC temperature for telemetry only. [OTA callbacks](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:765) log without charge inhibition. [reset/reconfigure MQTT handlers](../../../software/ups/src/UPS.ino:685) can reset/reconfigure the charger. All become relevant once CE control is added.

## 5. Smallest proposed implementation — not applied

Retain the existing circuit. Add a small V4-only charge-permission owner in the shared firmware, with explicit board configuration and no new remote charging controls. Other boards explicitly declare physical CE control absent; preserve their existing behavior.

1. Add explicit `CHG_ARM=GPIO0`, active-HIGH, and `expectedCells=4` for ups4. Before Serial, networking, I2C or callbacks, preload the output LOW and configure it as an output. Do not use open-drain HIGH: Q7 needs positive base drive. Keep diagnostic CE-test mode and actual battery-charge authorization separate, both default disabled for staged bring-up.
2. Use one owner for V4 EN_CHG and GPIO0 decisions. Minimal states can be INHIBITED, CONFIGURING, PERMITTED and FAULT; separate the requested permission from charger communication readiness and actual CHG_STAT. Existing legacy/profile helpers must not independently re-enable V4 charging. Refactor managed profile application to permit configuration/readback without automatic enable, retaining the existing behavior for older boards.
3. **Revoke first:** GPIO0 LOW before logs, error clearing, charger reset, reconfiguration, OTA start, detected input loss, invalid/stale sample, temperature failure or profile drift. Then best-effort EN_CHG=0. Put revocation before `setChargerReady`'s unchanged-state early return. No I2C success is required to release the hardware gate.
4. With GPIO0 LOW, explicitly write and freshly verify EN_CHG=0; verify BQ25798 identity and expected 4S, approved profile/VREG/recharge, ICHG, input limits and modes, TS_IGNORE=0, relevant TS/thermal/fault and safety-timer settings. Reapply/verify the required values even when the old initialization marker is set. Preserve the current explicit managed profile; its proposed 15.68 V target still requires owner/cell approval for the test and does not certify 80% SOC. A switch to FULL=16.8 V would require a separate decision and confirmation of four 4.2 V cells. Do not silently rewrite CELL to mask a mismatch.
5. Permit only from a completely successful fresh sample: approved pack/test authorization, expected pack configuration, valid battery voltage, qualified NTC reading within the actual cell's approved charging window, healthy charger statuses, and valid forward input (PG good, AC1 present, measured source consistent with the setup, EN_HIZ=0, EN_OTG=0, V4 EN_BACKUP=0). The capture's VBUS status code 8 alone must not reject a valid adapter when automatic type detection is disabled. Confirm source capacity and input limits separately.
6. While CE remains inhibited, set EN_CHG=1 and independently re-read/verify it and safety-critical settings. Only then drive GPIO0 HIGH as the final action. A failed read/write or changed qualification leaves GPIO0 LOW. At termination, allow the IC's approved native termination/recharge behavior without repeatedly toggling CE. On loss of qualification, revoke and return through complete configuration/qualification before a new enable.
7. Evaluate locally on a bounded safety-service cadence; check immediately at error/state-changing entry points. Define and measure I2C timeouts and sample age. Do not claim a cutoff bound based on the loop's 20 ms delay: I2C, DNS, MQTT and OTA can block. Network availability should not itself authorize charging. In this prototype, latch serious I2C/profile/temperature/pack faults off for owner inspection; normal input return may requalify only under the approved test mode. Avoid repetitive fault retries that toggle CE and restart safety timers.
8. Publish only read-only diagnostics such as requested permission, qualification reason, sample age and fault state. There is no CE feedback input: GPIO readback is not physical TP14 verification. Add focused host tests for operation ordering, every error exit, stale/partial reads, wrong CELL, marker skip, input return, temperature loss, OTA and the legacy/profile competing-enable paths. Build affected environments after an implementation is approved, then conduct the physical tests below.

**Residual limitation:** a charger-only reset while GPIO0 remains HIGH can restore defaults before firmware observes the change; a stalled MCU may leave it HIGH. Software polling and the existing disabled charger watchdog cannot guarantee a 200/500 mA ceiling. For this fitted BQ25798, qualify the pack for the documented 1 A default plus tolerance, or obtain a separate owner decision if a hard lower ceiling is required. This proposal adds no supervisor, latch or watchdog. GPIO0 must remain LOW across known reset/configuration operations; unexpected chip reset remains a measured/accepted limitation.

## 6. Proposed staged bench test — requires later authorization

**Stage A: demonstrate physical CE control with EN_CHG held at zero.** Prefer no battery, no external SYS module/load, and a controlled stable input. Confirm the actual driver population and unpowered continuity first. Add a deliberate diagnostic mode whose sole charge-register policy is EN_CHG=0; all normal profile/legacy/reconfigure paths must respect it. The present source could undo a one-off EN_CHG=0 write, so that is not a safe test arrangement.

- Confirm fresh EN_CHG=0 and idle charge status before every deliberate GPIO HIGH. No battery avoids depending solely on that register during reset tests. Hold GPIO LOW through any charger reset and reverify EN_CHG=0 afterward.
- Measure TP13 MAIN_3V3, TP8 MCU3V3, REGN at C3.1, D3/D4 cathode bias, GPIO0/R46.1, Q7 base, CHG_AND, Q6 collector, Q5 base, Q3 gate and TP14. Use safe resistor/pad access rather than probing between QFN pins. All scope grounds connect to PGND.
- Toggle GPIO LOW→HIGH→LOW under diagnostic control. Expect TP14 HIGH→≤0.4 V→HIGH; gate roughly 0→3.2→0 V. Confirm CE HIGH≥1.3 V after settling, zero charging, EN_CHG still zero, and no rail disturbance. Scope transitions; a DMM cannot exclude a boot pulse.
- Exercise reset/ROM boot, reboot and OTA entry while battery-free. Check MAIN-only, USB-only and USB/main transitions. Test true MCU power loss only with a safe existing means to remove its supply; reset is a separate test, and pulling USB is insufficient when MAIN supplies it. Do not alter the mux/isolation or inject power into GPIO to create the test.
- Explicitly observe a retained diagnostic HIGH during main-rail return with EN_CHG=0: the hardware may immediately pull CE LOW. The normal controller must prevent this stale permission. Stop at this checkpoint and review measured waveforms before battery charging.

**Stage B: pack approval before connection.** Record board/population, fitted IC/R5, cell manufacturer/type/chemistry/condition/capacity and permitted charge voltage/current/temperature. Measure all four individual cells, their sum and BMS pack terminals; compare balance and cell limits against the cell/BMS specifications. Confirm correct 4S BMS wiring, charge-port polarity, charge/discharge current ratings, functioning protection and balancing arrangement, wiring/fuse and insulation. Pack-average VBAT is insufficient. Do not use a BMS trip or deliberate cell overcharge as the charging stop test. Unknown cell/BMS limits or an out-of-limit/unbalanced cell means no charging test.

**Stage C: brief guarded low-current charging.** After Stage A review and separate owner approval, propose **200 mA ICHG** as an initial test setting, subject to actual cell approval; this is a test proposal, not a product requirement. Keep the normal 500 mA-versus-current-1000 mA decision separate. Set source/input limits from actual source capability and `Pinput ≳ Psystem + Vbattery × Icharge / efficiency`; a source current limit is not a battery-current limit. Use the already characterized nominal 20 V source arrangement, initially with unnecessary SYS loads disconnected.

- Attach the approved pack with source disabled and CE inhibited. Log each cell, pack voltage, NTC/contact temperature and ambient. Secure the NTC to the pack and compare with an independent contact thermometer. Resolve temperature qualification enough for the chosen room-temperature test; do not use the presently discrepant TDIE reading as the only thermometer.
- Verify all configured/read-back limits and permissions while CE is HIGH. Confirm ITERM/IPRECHG and charging timers too. Saved ITERM=200 mA equals the proposed initial ICHG, so this brief test must remain away from CV and does not qualify termination; choose a cell-approved termination setting in a later reviewed step if a full cycle is desired.
- Apply the controlled input, verify forward power, then perform the approved final enable sequence. Observe battery current with an independent instrument as well as ADC, positive charging direction, correct CHG_STAT, each individual cell, SYS/input stability and pack/BQ/inductor/FET/connector temperatures. Record an initial short interval (for example 30–60 s), review, then extend only if stable.
- Stop by GPIO LOW, verify TP14 rises and charge current ceases, then confirm EN_CHG=0. Test fault/input-loss inhibition under controlled conditions after normal stop succeeds. Do not live-short the I2C bus or deliberately open a charging BMS; use software fault injection first, and plan any physical fault fixture separately. Source removal alone leaves the battery as an energy source.
- Abort for any cell approaching its approved limit, unexpected current, rising/abnormal temperature, missing temperature data, I2C error, source/rail instability or failed CE response. Numerical temperature, cell-balance and current tolerances must come from the actual cell/BMS and agreed test setup before starting; they are not established by this investigation.
