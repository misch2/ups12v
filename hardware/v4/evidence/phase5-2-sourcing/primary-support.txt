L70@P0: 3 Description
L71@P0: The TCA9517 is a bidirectional buffer with level
L72@P0: shifting capabilities for I
L73@P0: 2C and SMBus systems. It
L74@P0: provides bidirectional voltage-level translation (uptranslation/down-translation) between low voltages
L75@P0: (down to 0.9 V) and higher voltages (2.7 V to 5.5 V)
L76@P0: in mixed-mode applications. This device enables I
L77@P0: 2C
L78@P0: and SMBus systems to be extended without
L79@P0: degradation of performance, even during level
L80@P0: shifting.
L81@P0: The TCA9517 buffers both the serial data (SDA) and
L82@P0: the serial clock (SCL) signals on the I
L83@P0: 2C bus, thus
L84@P0: allowing two buses of up to 400-pF bus capacitance
L85@P0: to be connected in an I
L86@P0: 2C application.
L87@P0: The TCA9517 has two types of drivers: A-side drivers
L88@P0: and B-side drivers. All inputs and I/Os are overvoltage tolerant to 5.5 V, even when the device is
L89@P0: unpowered (VCCB and/or VCCA = 0 V).

L157@P2: 5 Description (continued)
L158@P2: The type of buffer design on the B-side prevents it from being used in series with devices which use static
L159@P2: voltage offset. This is because these devices do not recognize buffered low signals as a valid low and do not
L160@P2: propagate it as a buffered low again.
L161@P2: The B-side drivers operate from 2.7 V to 5.5 V. The output low level for this internal buffer is approximately 0.5 V,
L162@P2: but the input voltage must be 70 mV or more below the output low level when the output internally is driven low.
L163@P2: The higher-voltage low signal is called a buffered low. When the B-side I/O is driven low internally, the low is not
L164@P2: recognized as a low by the input. This feature prevents a lockup condition from occurring when the input low
L165@P2: condition is released.
L166@P2: The A-side drivers operate from 0.9 V to 5.5 V and drive more current. They do not require the buffered low
L167@P2: feature (or the static offset voltage). This means that a low signal on the B-side translates to a nearly 0 V low on
L168@P2: the A-side, which accommodates smaller voltage swings of lower-voltage logic. The output pulldown on the Aside drives a hard low, and the input level is set at 0.3 × VCCA to accommodate the need for a lower low level in
L169@P2: systems where the low-voltage-side supply voltage is as low as 0.9 V.
L170@P2: The A-side of two or more TCA9517 s can be connected together, allowing many topographies (See Figure 8
L171@P2: and Figure 9 ), with the A-side as the common bus. Also, the A-side can be connected directly to any other buffer
L172@P2: with static- or dynamic-offset voltage. Multiple TCA9517 s can be connected in series, A-side to B-side, with no
L173@P2: buildup in offset voltage and with only time-of-flight delays to consider. The TCA9517 cannot be connected Bside to B-side, because of the buffered low voltage from the B-side. The B-side cannot be connected to a device
L174@P2: with rise time accelerators.
L175@P2: VCCA is only used to provide the 0.3 × VCCA reference to the A-side input comparators and for the power-gooddetect circuit. The TCA9517 logic and all I/Os are powered by the VCCB pin.
L176@P2: As with the standard I
L177@P2: 2C system, pullup resistors are required to provide the logic-high levels on the buffered
L178@P2: bus. The TCA9517 has standard open-drain configuration of the I
L179@P2: 2C bus. The size of these pullup resistors
L180@P2: depends on the system, but each side of the repeater must have a pullup resistor. The device is designed to
L181@P2: work with Standard mode and Fast mode I
L182@P2: 2C devices in addition to SMBus devices. Standard mode I2C devices
L183@P2: only specify 3 mA in a generic I
L184@P2: 2C system, where Standard mode devices and multiple masters are possible.
