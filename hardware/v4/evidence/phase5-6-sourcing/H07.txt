Product FAQ
1. What voltage rating, current capacity, and wire gauge ranges govern the JST XHP-2 housing?
The JST XHP-2 wire-to-board socket housing is rated for a maximum voltage of 250V AC/DC and a continuous current capacity of up to 3A AC/DC when used with AWG #22 conductors. It accepts compatible crimp socket contacts, including the SXH-001T-P0.6 and SXH-002T-P0.6 series, supporting wire sizes from AWG #30 to #22 (0.05–0.33 mm², depending on contact type) with insulation outer diameters ranging from 0.9 mm to 1.9 mm.
Read more
2. Which pin pitch, position count, housing material, and flammability ratings define the XHP-2?
The XHP-2 features a 2.5 mm pitch in a single-row, 2-position configuration. It is molded from natural white PA6 (Nylon 6) with a UL94V-0 flammability rating. The datasheet lists the housing dimensions for the XHP-2 as A = 2.5 mm, B = 5.7 mm, and C = 7.3 mm.
Read more
3. Which PCB headers and crimp contacts pair directly with the XHP-2 housing?
The XHP-2 receptacle housing is compatible with the 2-circuit JST XH-series through-hole PCB headers, including the B2B-XH-A top-entry and S2B-XH-A side-entry headers. It accepts SXH-001T-P0.6 and SXH-002T-P0.6 crimp contacts for wire termination.
Read more
4. What operating temperature limits, insulation resistance, and dielectric specifications apply to the XHP-2 housing?
The XHP-2 is specified for an operating temperature range of −25°C to +85°C, including temperature rise during current flow. The connector system provides an initial contact resistance of 10 mΩ maximum, an insulation resistance of at least 1,000 MΩ, and a withstanding voltage of 1,000 VAC for one minute.
Read more
5. How is the XHP-2 housing used during PCB assembly?
The XHP-2 is a cable-side crimp housing and is not mounted directly to a printed circuit board. Instead, it mates with compatible 2-circuit JST XH-series through-hole PCB headers, such as the B2B-XH-A and S2B-XH-A, while the electrical connection to the wire is made using compatible SXH-series crimp contacts.
