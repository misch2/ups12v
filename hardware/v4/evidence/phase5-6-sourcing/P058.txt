Introduction
AI Translation

ESP32-C3-MINI-1 and ESP32-C3-MINI-1U are general-purpose Wi-Fi and low-power Bluetooth modules featuring a compact form factor and rich peripheral interfaces, suitable for smart home, industrial automation, healthcare, consumer electronics, and other applications.

Features
AI Translation
Integrated ESP32-C3FH4 or ESP32-C3FN4 chip, RISC-V 32-bit single-core microprocessor, up to 160MHz clock speed, 384KB ROM, 400KB SRAM, 8KB RTC SRAM, 4MB embedded flash
IEEE 802.11b/g/n, operating frequency 2412~2484MHz, 20MHz and 40MHz bandwidth, 1T1R mode, data rate up to 150Mbps, WMM, frame aggregation, immediate block ACK, fragmentation and defragmentation, TXOP, automatic Beacon monitoring, 4x virtual Wi-Fi interfaces, Station, SoftAP, Station+SoftAP, and promiscuous modes, 802.11mc FTM
Bluetooth 5 LE, Bluetooth Mesh, data rates: 125Kbps, 500Kbps, 1Mbps, 2Mbps, advertising extensions, multiple advertising, channel selection, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth coexistence
Peripherals: GPIO, SPI, UART, I2C, I2S, IR remote control, LED PWM controller, general DMA controller, TWAI controller (CAN 2.0 compatible), USB serial/JTAG controller, temperature sensor, SAR ADC, general-purpose timers, watchdog timers
40MHz integrated crystal oscillator
Antenna options: on-board PCB antenna (ESP32-C3-MINI-1) or external antenna via connector (ESP32-C3-MINI-1U)
Operating voltage 3.0~3.6V, operating temperature: -40~85°C and -40~105°C variants
Testing: HTOL, HTSL, uHAST, TCT, ESD, Latch-up
