L1374:

L1377:   * 2. Is the RC0402FR-074K7L Compatible With Equivalent 4.7kΩ 0402 Resistors?

L1380:   * 3. What Applications Is the RC0402FR-074K7L Electronic Component Commonly Used In?
L1381: This 0402 SMD resistor is widely used in applications such as consumer electronics, communication devices, industrial controls, IoT products, and embedded systems. Its small footprint and reliable performance make it ideal for signal conditioning, voltage division, pull-up/pull-down networks, and current-limiting applications.

L1383:   * 4. What Are the Key Specifications of the YAGEO RC0402FR-074K7L Resistor?
