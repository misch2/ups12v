L926: ## Product Overview
L927: 
L928: C3216X5R1V226M160AC is a C series multilayer ceramic chip capacitor. The monolithic structure ensures superior mechanical strength and high reliability. Also, outstanding frequency characteristics such as low ESR and low ESL are provided owing to the simpler structure than other capacitors. Typical applications include general electronic equipment, mobile devices, servers, PCs, tablets, and power supply circuits.
L929:   * Superior mechanical strength and high reliability due to the monolithic structure
L930:   * Outstanding frequency characteristics such as low ESR and low ESL by the simple structure
L931:   * Low self-heating value and high resistance to ripple on account of the low ESR
L932:   * No polarity
L933:   * 22μF capacitance
L934:   * Capacitance tolerance is ±20%
L935:   * 35V DC voltage rating
L936:   * Operating temperature range from -55 to 85°C
L937:   * 3.2mm product length
L938:   * 1.6mm product width
