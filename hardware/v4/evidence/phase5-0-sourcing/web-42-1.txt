## Product Overview

C3216X5R1V226M160AC is a C series multilayer ceramic chip capacitor. The monolithic structure ensures superior mechanical strength and high reliability. Also, outstanding frequency characteristics such as low ESR and low ESL are provided owing to the simpler structure than other capacitors. Typical applications include general electronic equipment, mobile devices, servers, PCs, tablets, and power supply circuits.
